# The first lab becomes an essay

The positioning review's verdict was that the gap is prose, not features — ~190
words of connective explanation per lab, against LearnOpenGL's several thousand
on a single chapter. Arguments that were real and correctly ordered existed as
preset labels with the sentences between them unwritten. This is the prototype
of the fix, on lab 1.

## What we built

**A form: prose with single-control figures.** Two new pieces. `Prose` sets a
readable measure and typography for essay text. `Figure` is a canvas, at most
one control, and a caption written after the fact — if it needs two controls it
is two figures. The instrument at the foot of the page still has all sixteen
controls and answers every question at once, which is exactly why it cannot
teach any single one.

**Lab 1 rewritten as an essay**, 391 words to 1,472, with five figures:
translation moving one column, rotation leaving the last column alone, the
basis vectors as literally the first three columns, a non-uniform scale, and
the two composition orders side by side driven by one slider. Five linkable
section headings, which is the point — a search engine cannot index a slider
and a lecturer cannot assign a canvas.

Every figure drives the lab's own `createScene`, so a figure cannot drift away
from the instrument below it; they are the same renderer with different controls
exposed.

## What we checked

The order figure's claim is arithmetic, not assertion: T·R leaves the cube at
(2.20, 0, 0) — turned where it stood, then moved — and R·T at (1.26, 0, −1.80),
swung around the origin. Verified against the matrix library before the sentence
describing it was written.

## What we fixed

Headings rendered accent-blue, because the `[&_a]:link-accent` rule in `Prose`
was catching the anchor each heading carries for its own `#` affordance. Scoped
to paragraphs and lists.

## What we fixed, after looking at it

The essay column was centred inside the page's `max-w-6xl` main while the lab's
own title is left-aligned, so the prose started 234px to the right of the h1
above it and read as a different page pasted in. Left-aligned to match the
header. Only visible by opening the page — the build, the tests and the smoke
suite were all green with it.

## Key decisions

- **One lab first, not all ten.** The form is a bet — prose interleaved with
  isolated figures, in Ciechanowski's grammar. Better to see it working on the
  entry point than to write 15,000 words in a shape nobody has looked at.
- **Reusing the lab's scene rather than writing figure-sized ones.** A figure
  that renders differently from the instrument it introduces would be worse than
  no figure.
